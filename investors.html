A Massive Market Waiting For Innovation

The equine industry represents one of the most resilient specialty consumer markets in North America.

The United States horse industry contributes approximately $122 Billion annually to the American economy through agriculture, sport, recreation, breeding, boarding, veterinary care, and equestrian events.

Millions of horses require seasonal protection from:

  • Horse Flies
  • Stable Flies
  • Mosquitoes
  • Gnats
  • Ticks
  • Midges

For owners and professionals, fly protection isn't optional— it's part of routine horse care.
